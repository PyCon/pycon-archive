Dear technical leaders, how are you thinking about the future of your teams?

Room 201B | 11 a.m. - noon

The jury is out on what the future of code looks like. What we do know is that junior developers are having a pretty difficult time, and the job market really sucks for them right now. This is true in our field, and beyond, it's being called "The Apprenticeship Crisis". Juniors are struggling to find work, which means that they are struggling to build the skills and experience they would need to succeed in this career in the long term. Sucks for them... Do you think it'll suck for your organisation in the future? I'm pretty convinced (maybe you are too) that bootcamps and universities are not going to save the day here. Organisations that believe they'll still have a need for technical talent in the future are going to need to plan for that future. If you are a technical leader, I have some questions for you: You're going to need to place some bets and make some plans: - What do you think your team will look like in 2-5 years? How about 10? What horizon are you considering? - If you are concerned about the apprenticeship crisis, what are you doing about it? Do you have a plan? Let's talk
