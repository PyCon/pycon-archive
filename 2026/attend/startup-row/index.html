Startup Row

Startup Row is where early-stage companies go to show off what they’re doing with Python at PyCon US.

At PyCon US, we reserve a special place for startup companies. Startup Row companies receive complimentary conference passes, booth space on Thursday evening for the Opening Reception (May 14, 2026) as well as on Friday and Saturday of the main conference (May 15 - 16, 2026), access to the Job Fair (May 17, 2026), and all the best of what PyCon US has to offer. PyCon US 2026 is in Long Beach, California, and we hope to see you there!

TLDR:

Applications for Startup Row are now closed.

If you haven’t already, register for a PyCon US account, make sure you’re logged in, and submit your application to Startup Row at PyCon US here.

Applications for booth space on Startup Row at PyCon US will now open up on December 8th by popular demand; this coincides with opening the PyCon US 2026 CFP. That way, any founder or team member who also wants to submit a talk or workshop proposal can do so before the CFP deadline of December 19th.

The final deadline for submitting an application for booth space on Startup Row at PyCon US 2026 is January 30, 2026. All applications will be reviewed together, and consideration for a spot on Startup Row at PyCon US 2026 is independent from the CFP review process.

Does your startup qualify? Here are the criteria:

  • You use Python somewhere (anywhere, but the more the better) in your tech or business stack
  • Your startup is 2.5 years old, or less, at time of applying (if you had a major pivot or delayed product launch, measure from there)
  • You have 25 or fewer full-time employees, including founders, at the time of applying
  • You or your company will fund travel and accommodation to PyCon US in Long Beach, CA
  • You have not previously presented your current company on Startup Row at PyCon US (Previous applicants and alumni founders working on new projects are welcome to apply!)
  • You are able to attend and staff your booth on the following days:
    • Thursday, May 14, 2026: Opening Reception
    • Friday, May 15 & Saturday, May 16, 2026: Main conference expo hall days
  • Please note: if your company is selected to be featured on Startup Row, a $400 USD refundable deposit is required to secure your booth space and Job Fair table (if opting-in to attend the Job Fair). If all events are attended and staffed by your company, the $400 deposit will be refunded after the event.
    • Participation in the Job Fair on Sunday, May 17, 2026, is optional. However, if you opt-in for the Job Fair and do not attend, the $400 deposit will not be returned.

So, what’s in it for you and your startup?

  • Two conference passes (you’re welcome to stay for the post-conference sprints, too); discounted passes for additional team members are available
  • Booth space on Startup Row in the Expo Hall on the evening of May 14th for the Opening Reception and on the main exhibit days, May 15th and 16th, 2026.
  • Optional table at the PyCon US Job Fair on Sunday, May 17, 2026
  • Eternal glory

How many companies have presented on Startup Row over the years?

If accepted, your startup joins a strong group of Startup Row alumni companies, which include the likes of: Coalition (🦄 SR’18), Ponder Data (acq’d by Snowflake, SR’23), Chainguard (🦄 SR’22), Anvil (SR‘16), and more than 150 others since Startup Row became “a thing” in 2011.

A program like Startup Row at PyCon US is only as robust as its community, and the past 5 years have been exceptionally good:

  • Out of the 40 companies featured on SR since 2020, two of them are now valued at over $1 billion
  • This same population of companies, in the time after appearing on Startup Row, have raised at least $950M in venture capital funding
  • Out of those 40 companies, 4 have been acquired (Noteable → Confluent. Bit.io → Databricks. Ponder Data → Snowflake. Resurface Labs → Greylog.)
  • Besides US-based companies, Startup Row participants have flown in from Mexico, Canada, Germany, and the United Kingdom, among other places

What do other founders have to say about their experience?

It’s a great opportunity to connect with the Python community. But don’t take our words for it. Here is what some have to say about their participation in Startup Row:
