Onsite Information

Long Beach Convention & Entertainment Center

The Long Beach Convention & Entertainment Center is located at 300 E Ocean Blvd, Long Beach, CA 90802. We recommend all attendees enter through the Main Entrance on Pine Avenue.

  • Long Beach Convention Center Floorplan
  • Directions and Parking Information
  • LBCC Accessibility Information

  • The Google Play Store

Registration

Registration will open at 7:30 am PT on Wednesday, May 13th. It will be located in the Promenade Lobby, on the ground floor of the Convention Center.

When arriving, please enter through the Main Entrance on Pine Avenue. Please note that you must check in and have your badge printed to enter the Expo Hall.

Please also note that the registration desk will NOT be open during the post-conference Sprint days (Monday, May 18th-Tuesday, May 19). If you are planning to attend any of the Sprint days, you MUST check-in before Sprints start at the conference registration desk, which closes at 1 PM PT on Sunday, May 16th.

Please have your photo ID ready when standing in the queue.

Hours:

  • Wednesday, May 13th: 7:30 am - 6:00 pm PT
  • Thursday, May 14th: 7:30 am - 6:00 pm PT
  • Friday, May 15th: 7:30 am - 5:00 pm PT
  • Saturday, May 16th: 8:00 am - 5:00 pm PT
  • Sunday, May 17th: 8:00 am - 1:00 pm PT

Information Desk

If you have any questions on-site, such as directions, lost and found, or scheduling, head to the Information Desk in the Promenade Lobby, by the Main Entrance, where volunteers can help answer them.

Hours:

  • Wednesday, May 13th: 7:30 am - 5:00 pm PT
  • Thursday, May 14th: 7:30 am - 5:00 pm PT
  • Friday, May 15th: 7:30 am - 4:00 pm PT
  • Saturday, May 16th: 8:00 am - 4:00 pm PT
  • Sunday, May 17th: 8:00 am - 12:00 pm PT

Swag Pickup

Any PyCon US conference T-shirts, PyLadies and Charlas t-shirts ordered during the registration process will be available for pickup at the Swag Pickup counter in the Expo Hall located in Expo Hall AB. Below is the pick up schedule with days and times.

Hours:

  • Friday, May 15th: 9:00 am – 5:00 pm PT (Pre-order pickup)
  • Saturday, May 16th: 9:00 am – 1:30 pm PT (Pre-order pickup)
  • Sunday, May 17th: 10:00 am - 11:00 am PT (Pre-order pickup)
  • Extra T-shirts will be available in exchange for a donation on Sunday, May 17th from 11:00 am - 1:00 pm PT

Special Meal Pickup

If you provided special dietary requests when filling out your attendee profile, such as Kosher, food allergies, etc., please find a Savor catering staff member at each meal function who will assist you in picking up your special meal or help you identify options that are free of allergens.

Hours:

  • Thursday, May 14th: Opening Reception 5:00 pm - 7:00 pm PT
  • Friday, May 15th: 8:00 am - 6:00 pm PT
  • Saturday, May 16th: 8:00 am - 4:30 pm PT

Job Fair & Community Showcase and Poster Presentations (Hall AB)

Attendee Quiet Room

The Attendee Quiet Room is a safe place for anyone who needs a break or a quiet place for whatever reason. The Attendee Quiet room is located on the second floor of the Promenade area, in Room VIP A&B.

The Quiet Room is open:

  • Thursday, May 14th: 9:00 AM PT - 5:00 PM PT
  • Friday, May 15th - Sunday, May 17th: 7:00 AM - 5:00 PM PT

Wellness Room/Nursing Suite

There is a Wellness Room/Nursing Suite available in the Promenade Lobby, across from the Registration Desk, from Wednesday, May 13th-Sunday May 17th.

Gender Neutral Restrooms

There is a gender neutral restroom designated on each floor, in the Expo Hall, and in the Seaside rooms. Please see the locations marked in purple on the detailed floor plans.

First Aid

The first aid station is in different locations depending on where most attendees will be. Please see the locations and hours on these maps.

No Luggage Check

There is no Luggage Check available at the Convention Center this year. You can ask your hotel or your lodging accommodation if they provide luggage check.
