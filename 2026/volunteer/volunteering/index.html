Volunteering

Have a couple of hours to give? PyCon US is, in large part, organized and run by volunteers from the Python community. We're aiming to fill over 350 onsite volunteer hours to help make sure everything runs smoothly and would greatly appreciate your help!

Please read about the role details and responsibilities below and follow the links to sign up!

How to sign up: Please log in to your profile on the PyCon US website, be sure you are registered for the conference, and update your contact info so we can be in contact. Follow the links below to sign up for specific roles. To sign up for an open volunteer shift, click the [+] in the corresponding box. After signing up, you will see your shifts on your dashboard! Your dashboard is also where you can “opt out” of shifts after signing up if needed.

Volunteer Role Details and Responsibilities

Registration (click here to sign up)

When: May 13 - 17, 2026

Someone needs to say "Welcome! What is your name?", help attendees to print their name tag (if needed), and hand them their badge holder and lanyard. This is a great way to meet people and answer basic questions. If this is your first PyCon US, it's a good way to get into volunteering and network!

Please report to the Registration Counters in the Promenade Lobby at your scheduled time.

Information Desk (click here to sign up)

When: May 13 - 17, 2026

There are always lots of questions that need answering and directions that need giving!

Please report to the Information Counter in the Promenade Lobby at your scheduled time.

Green Room (click here to sign up)

When: May 13 - 17, 2026

Green Room volunteers coordinate the Session Runners and Chairs. They check-in volunteers and speakers, take charge of timing of talks, use a walkie-talkie, and reassure speakers before their talk. Please report to Room 203C at the time of your shift.

If you sign up for this role, please do your absolute best to avoid canceling or worst case not showing up, so that we can make sure we have coverage for all the necessary time slots.

Tutorial Hosts (click here to sign up)

When: May 13 - 14, 2026

We're looking for one or two Tutorial Hosts for each tutorial to help welcome and scan in participants as they arrive, be sure they're in the right tutorial, give them any printed handouts, and ask them if they need help setting up their laptop, etc.

Pro tip: Sign up to help with a tutorial you would like to attend, and save your seat first!

Please report to Room 203C at least 30 minutes before the tutorial – that is 30 minutes before the time slot you sign up for!

Wayfinders (click here to sign up)

When: May 14 - 17, 2026

Be a welcoming face and help attendees navigate the convention center and find their way to different rooms, tutorials and sessions. This is a standing role and a good fit for someone who is chatty and feels comfortable actively asking anyone if they need help finding their way around!

Please report to the Staff Office in Room 203AB 15 minutes before your scheduled time.

Swag Setup (click here to sign up)

When: May 14, 2026

This is a physical role. We will be moving boxes and unboxing swag, getting ready to hand them out.

Meet at the Swag Table (back of Expo Hall AB) at your scheduled time.

Swag Handout (click here to sign up)

When: May 15 - May 17, 2026

Help distribute stickers and t-shirts; especially during that busy first morning of the conference.

Meet at the Swag Table (back of Expo Hall AB) at your scheduled time.

Captioner Runner (click here to sign up)

When: May 15 - 17, 2026

Captioner Runners help our onsite captioning team navigate from the General Session to their Talk room (either Ballroom A or Hall C) and vice versa by wheeling their equipment and guiding them through the crowd during the break. Our captioners only have a few minutes to move through the crowd of attendees after a session ends and get set up before the next begins and need your help!

Instructions on where to report and when will be emailed to you closer to the conference.

Session Staff (read more details and sign up here)

When: May 15 - May 17, 2026

  • Session Chairs sit in the room where speakers and talks are taking place, introduce the speaker, manage the time by holding signs, and facilitate the question and answer period. Must have a smartphone and Discord.
  • If you sign up for this role, please do your absolute best to avoid canceling or worst case not showing up, so that we can make sure we have coverage for all the necessary time slots.
  • Session Runners (no actual running required) accompany the speaker from the Green Room to the room of their talk. Helps or waits for the AV team to hook up the laptop for presentation and makes sure everything is working. Free to watch the talk and then return to the Green Room for the next speaker.

PyLadies Auction Helpers (click here to sign up)

When: May 16, 2026 (Saturday Evening)

  • Setup: The PyLadies Auction needs staff to help for the event! Important to note: A PyLadies Auction ticket must be purchased to volunteer. To save your place at the Auction, add the PyLadies Auction to your PyCon US registration via your dashboard. Meet in the Staff Office in Room 203AB. Help shuttle auction items to PyLadies Auction room and arrange for viewing. Please report at the time of the start of your shift on Saturday, May 16, 2026.
  • Runner: The PyLadies Auction needs staff to help for the event! Important to note: A PyLadies Auction ticket must be purchased to volunteer. To save your place at the Auction, add the PyLadies Auction to your PyCon US registration via your dashboard. Meet at PyLadies Auction in Seaside Ballroom AB. Walk items around the ballroom floor for attendees to get a better look at what is being bid on. Please report at the time of the start of your shift on Saturday, May 16, 2026

PSF Booth (click here to sign up)

When: May 14 - May 16, 2026

Before the Opening Reception, PSF Booth Volunteers help set up the PSF’s booth in the Expo Hall. During the Opening Reception and main conference days, PSF Booth Volunteers work with PSF staff and Board members to help answer questions about the PSF that booth visitors have, after a very brief training and with resources and supervisors available. After the main conference days, PSF Booth Volunteers help break down the booth and pack up anything that needs to go into storage. (We’ve heard rumors that Booth volunteers also get a special sticker, but we can neither confirm nor deny.) Please report at the time of the start of your shift to the PSF Booth in Expo Hall AB.

Code of Conduct Committee

When: May 13 - May 20, 2026

PyCon US is a community conference where we want every attendee to feel welcome and have an enjoyable, fulfilling experience. We are looking for volunteers to help the PyCon US team enforce the PyCon US Code of Conduct as well as to take Code of Conduct reports as needed to help us ensure that all attendees feel safe and respected during their time at PyCon US.

If you are interested in volunteering for this committee, please fill out the short application here.
