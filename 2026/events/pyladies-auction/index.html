PyLadies Auction

The Fourteenth PyLadies Auction will be held in-person during PyCon US 2026 in Long Beach, California!

Please join us for an evening of fun and refreshments while supporting the PyLadies community. So, come with a good sense of 'bidding' humor (bidding is not required). The format of the auction will be live bidding.

  • Date: Saturday, May 16, 2026
  • Time: 6:45 pm PST
  • Location: Seaside Ballroom AB at the Long Beach Convention & Entertainment Center

The Entry cover charge of $50.00 includes dinner and a drink ticket, while the Supporter cover charge of $100.00 includes dinner, a drink ticket, and a $50.00 contribution to PyLadies. We have increased the cover charge this year to help offset rising food and venue costs. Everyone who attends will also have access to the cashless bar.

To save your place at the Auction, add the PyLadies Auction to your PyCon US registration via your dashboard. Last year we sold out quickly, so if you’d like to go, reserve your ticket soon!

Please Note

The main entrance is not open for the PyLadies Auction. Attendees go to the ground level and walk on East Seaside Way to the Seaside Lobby entrance (Ground Level!) You MUST bring your badge to gain entry.

Donations

If you are interested in donating an item to the auction, please complete our donation form.

This form asks for required information around the person or organization donating, item specifics, and logistics. You will need to indicate if you will ship the item in advance or deliver it in person. You will receive shipping information once you complete the form. The deadline to drop off donations in person is May 16th, 2026, at 4 pm PST (that’s just about 3 hours before the event!).

If you’d like to learn more about what to donate, check out our blog post. Please note that we cannot accept donations of guns, alcohol, cars, real estate, or houses. Also, we aren’t accepting signed books this year. We may decline other gifts that are inappropriate or impractical at the PSF’s discretion (for example, sorry, no real pythons!).

Sponsors have the opportunity to donate auction items for the event. Contributing sponsors will be highlighted in event marketing materials and during the event.

If you have any questions about the auction or are interested in participating, please contact our Auction Coordinators.

About PyLadies

PyLadies is an international mentorship group with a focus on helping more women become active participants and leaders in the Python open source community. PyLadies’ mission is to promote, educate, and advance a diverse Python community through outreach, education, conferences, events, and social gatherings.

PyLadies aims to provide a friendly support network for women and a bridge to the larger Python world. Anyone with an interest in Python is encouraged to participate! Check out local meetups around the world.
