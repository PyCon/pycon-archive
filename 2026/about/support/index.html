Q: How do I stay up to date with PyCon US announcements?

A: To stay up to date on all the latest news and important announcements, be sure to create an account here on this website and opt-in to receive PyCon US News and subscribe to the PyCon US Blog. You should also follow us on:

  • X
  • Bluesky
  • Mastodon
  • LinkedIn

Q: Who should I contact if I have a registration question?

A: For all registration-specific questions, please email pycon-reg@python.org.

Q: Who should I contact if an error is detected on the PyCon US website?

A: Email pycon-site@python.org with the details

Q: Who should I contact for general help with features about PyCon US?

A: Many answers can be found on the PyCon US FAQ page (coming soon). If you can not find your answer there email pycon-reg@python.org with your question

Q: Why did I not receive the verification email when creating an account?

A: Most often, the verification email is caught up in spam filters. Check the spam folder or contact the manager of the mail server.

Q: Who should I contact for specific event questions?

  • Elaine Wong, PyCon US Chair - pycon-staff@python.org
  • Jon Banafato, PyCon US Co-Chair - pycon-staff@python.org
  • Olivia Sauls, Program Director - olivia@python.org
  • Jaime Barrera, Community Events Coordinator - jaime@python.org
  • Loren Crary, Deputy Executive Director / Sponsorship - loren@python.org
